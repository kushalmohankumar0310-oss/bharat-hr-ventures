Our Core Services

Comprehensive solutions for workforce protection & statutory compliance

ESI (Employees' State Insurance)

In India, ESI is a self-financing social security and health insurance scheme for workers. Managed by the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), it safeguards employees earning up to ₹21,000 per month against financial hardships caused by sickness, maternity, or employment-related injuries.

  • ESI Registration & Compliance
  • Cashless Medical Benefits support
  • Claims processing & coordination

PF (Provident Fund)

PF stands for Provident Fund. Managed by the EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation), it is a government-managed retirement savings scheme where a portion of an employee's salary is set aside each month to provide financial security after retirement.

  • EPF Registration & setup
  • Monthly returns and filing
  • Withdrawal & transfer support
